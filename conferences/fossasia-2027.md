## Submission Constraints

### Character/Word Limits
- **Title:** No explicit limit (keep concise)
- **Abstract:** No explicit limit (complete description strongly encouraged)
- **Description:** Be as detailed as possible — critical for selection

### Speaker Limits
- Max proposals per person: No stated limit

- Open source focus required
- No purely commercial/sales pitches

- Blind review: No

## What Reviewers Look For (Score High)

- Complete, detailed session description
- Clear open source connection
- Practical demos or hands-on elements
- Specific tools, code, or architectures mentioned
- Relevance to Asian tech community
- Novel approaches or unique perspectives
- Community contribution or collaboration stories

## Red Flags (Score Low / Reject)

- Vague or incomplete descriptions
- Pure product pitches with no open source substance
- Generic content available elsewhere
- No clear takeaway for attendees
- Missing open source angle

## Speaker Requirements

- Arrive prepared with complete presentation/materials

- For workshops: prepare hands-on materials/setup instructions

## Tips & Best Practices

- Submit early — rolling acceptance means first-come advantage
- Be specific about what attendees will learn or build
- Include demo plans if applicable
- Mention any prior speaking experience or project credentials
- If organizing a developer room or track, reach out early
- Description quality matters more than title cleverness
