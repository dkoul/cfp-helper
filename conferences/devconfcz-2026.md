## Submission Constraints

### Character/Word Limits
- **Title:** Max 100 characters
- **Abstract:** Max 1000 characters (including spaces)

### Speaker Limits
- **Max 3 proposals** per person (across all types; co-speaker credits count)
- **Max 2 speakers** per submission (exceptions for panels, justify in Notes)
- **Max 1 accepted talk (35 min)** per speaker — other formats can be accepted alongside

### Content Restrictions
- No PII in abstract (blind review)
- Completed slides not required at submission
- Proposals editable until CfP closes

### Review Process
- **Blind review** in first phase (anonymized proposals)
- Full-view review with speaker identity in second phase
- Track captains prioritize in third phase
- CfP Committee makes final selection
- Each track has 3-7 subject-matter expert reviewers

## What Reviewers Look For (Score High)

- **Original content** or original presentation approach
- **Technical, community/project-oriented** content
- **New or planned work** — emerging topics (1-2 year horizon)
- **Live demos and audience interaction** — explicitly encouraged
- **Open source / community angle** — fits the DevConf audience
- **Clear audience value** — attendees gain something concrete
- **Excitement factor** — would the reviewer want to attend?
- **Content depth matches duration** — 35 min should have 35 min of substance

## Red Flags (Score Low / Reject)

- Broad overview sessions lacking depth
- Marketing, sales pitches, or product promotions
- Stale or purely retrospective content
- No demos, no interaction, no concrete takeaways
- Mismatch between content depth and session length
- Generic proposals not tailored to DevConf audience
- **PII in abstract** (names, titles, companies) — violates blind review

## Tips & Best Practices

### Abstract Structure
- Write it like a trailer, not a table of contents
- Open with a hook that creates tension or challenges assumptions
- Include at least one concrete, quantified claim
- Promise a specific deliverable (tool, framework, workflow, mental model)
- Mention live demo if applicable
- End with a clear "you'll leave with..." statement
- Stay under 1000 characters — every word must earn its place
- **No PII** — no names, titles, or company names (blind review)

### Notes Field
- Notes are organizer-only, not public
- Include session outline with time allocations
- Describe target audience and prerequisites
- Explain what makes you qualified
- Mention any tools or demos you'll bring
- Keep consistent with abstract claims
- Don't contradict the abstract
